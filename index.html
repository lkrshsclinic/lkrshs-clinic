When to Come to the Clinic vs. Get Emergency Care

Quick reference so students and staff know what to do right away.

Visit the School Clinic For:

  • Mild fever, sore throat, cough, or cold symptoms
  • Minor scrapes, bruises, nosebleeds, or small burns
  • Mild cramps, nausea, or stress/fatigue during the day
  • Scheduled checkups, clearances, or inhaler/daily care check-ins

Get Immediate Emergency Help For:

  • Trouble breathing, severe allergic reactions, or swelling of lips/face
  • Chest pain, fainting, confusion, or severe head injury
  • Heavy bleeding or suspected broken bones
  • Any crisis where immediate hospital care is needed
